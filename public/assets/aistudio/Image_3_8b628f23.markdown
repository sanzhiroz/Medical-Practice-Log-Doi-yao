## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, reliable, and ergonomically precise digital environment for public healthcare practitioners, clinical staff, and regional administrators operating under the Chiang Mai Provincial Administrative Organization (à¸­à¸šà¸ˆ. à¹€à¸Šà¸µà¸¢à¸‡à¹ƒà¸«à¸¡à¹ˆ). Designed specifically for decentralized nodes such as Node Doi Yao, the portal balances official administrative dignity with the rapid, zero-friction usability required in clinical practice logging and field reporting.

The aesthetic follows an **Authoritative Modern Clinical** movement:
- **Tone:** Professional, dependable, clean, and meticulously organized.
- **Visual Weight:** Restrained and structured; prioritizing data clarity, legibility under varied lighting conditions (clinic desks, tablets in rural nodes), and visual composure.
- **Hierarchy:** Strong contrast separating systemic institutional elements (official headers, validation states, node indicators) from high-velocity operational inputs (patient intake counts, diagnostic entries, transfer manifests).

## Colors

The palette establishes clinical authority and clarity through deep maritime blues balanced by aerated cyan tints and crisp clinical surfaces:

- **Primary (`#0F2D6B` / `#1E3A8A`):** Deep Navy represents institutional governance, administrative permanence, and clinical authority. Used for top-level navigation, key structural headers, primary actions, and formal modal banners.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`):** Clinical Cyan provides focus, active states, key interactive indicators, and primary data highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` with background tint `#E0F2FE`):** Soft Sky Blue serves as contextual accents, information badges, selected table rows, and active filter pills.
- **Neutral & Text (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate Ink delivers uncompromising typographic legibility for Thai and Latin character sets.
- **Surface & Structural Borders:** `#FFFFFF` canvas with structural borders defined by subtle slate (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`) to maintain clean segmentation without visual clutter.
- **Semantic Signals:**
  - Success: `#059669` / Surface: `#ECFDF5` (Completed records, verified logs)
  - Warning / Pending: `#D97706` / Surface: `#FFFBEB` (Pending doctor review, incomplete triage)
  - Critical / Alert: `#DC2626` / Surface: `#FEF2F2` (Emergency referrals, system warnings)

## Typography

Typographic execution blends the architectural precision of **Prompt** for Thai/Latin display headers with the neutral metric rigor of **Inter** (paired with systematic Thai fallbacks such as Sarabun or Thonburi) for tabular data, complex log inputs, and technical metrics.

- **Headlines (Prompt):** Geometric yet accessible, providing structure to node administrative designations, section cards, and dashboard metrics.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Highly legible at small scales (12pxâ€“14px), essential for dense medical logs, ICD-10 codes, HN/VN references, and timestamps.
